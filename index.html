💸 kharcha - Turn Bank SMS Into a Clear Spending Ledger

⬇ Download kharcha for Windows

kharcha is a private spending ledger that reads the bank messages already sitting in your phone and turns them into a tidy, readable record of where your money goes. No subscriptions, no data selling, no complicated spreadsheets. Install it on Windows, open it once, and let it do the boring part for you.

Most people do not lose track of their money because they are careless. They lose track because writing down every purchase is tedious. kharcha removes that tedium by using the bank SMS alerts you already receive after every card swipe, transfer, or bill payment. It reads those messages, picks out the amount, the merchant, and the date, and files them into a single ledger you can scroll through in seconds.

🔍 What kharcha Actually Does

Think of kharcha as a very patient assistant that never gets tired of bookkeeping. When a bank message arrives saying you spent money at a shop or received a refund, kharcha notices it, extracts the important pieces, and adds a new line to your ledger. You never type a number. You never open a spreadsheet. You just check the ledger whenever you want to know how the month is going.

Everything happens privately. Your ledger lives on your own device. Nothing is uploaded anywhere unless you deliberately switch on the optional encrypted backup, which stores a scrambled copy on Render so you can restore your history if you change machines. If you never turn that on, your spending history stays exactly where it was created.

✨ Why People Keep Using It

  • Automatic entry: Bank SMS alerts become ledger lines without any typing from you.
  • One clear list: Every debit and credit in a single, chronological view instead of a dozen separate message threads.
  • Monthly totals: See how much you spent in the current month and compare it with the month before.
  • Spending categories: Group similar purchases together so the biggest drains on your budget become obvious.
  • Private by design: Your financial history is not sent to advertisers or analytics companies.
  • Optional encrypted backup: Turn on a secure Render backup when you want a safety net, and turn it off when you do not.
  • Lightweight build: The app is built with Expo, which keeps it small, fast to start, and simple to update.
  • Readable on any screen: Large amounts, dates, and merchant names are never squeezed into unreadable cells.

🚀 Your First Five Minutes With kharcha

Once kharcha opens for the first time, it will look almost empty. That is normal. There is nothing to show until it has some messages to work with. The setup below walks you through the first session so you are not left staring at a blank screen.

Step one: allow message access

kharcha needs to read your bank alerts to build the ledger. The first time you open the app, it will ask for permission to read messages. Accept the request. If you accidentally tap "no", you can turn the permission back on later in your device settings under the app's permissions section.

Step two: let the first scan run

The app now reads through your existing bank alerts and picks out the ones that look like spending or income. Depending on how many messages you have, this can take seconds or a couple of minutes. A progress indicator shows that work is happening, so do not close the window.

Step three: review the first entries

After the scan, you will see a list of transactions. Each line shows an amount, a short description, and the date. Scroll through the first few. If something looks wrong, tap it and correct the details. kharcha learns from these corrections, so the list gets more accurate the more you use it.

Step four: set your categories

Categories are simply labels such as food, transport, bills, or shopping. Assign a few to your most frequent merchants. Once a merchant has a category, kharcha applies it automatically every time that merchant appears again.

Step five: check your monthly total

Open the summary view to see how much has gone out this month and how much has come in. This single number is usually the moment the app clicks for new users: it is your whole month, in one place, without a single minute of typing.

🔐 Privacy and the Optional Encrypted Backup

Your spending history is personal, and kharcha treats it that way. Everything is stored locally on the machine where you run it. There is no advertising network inside the app, no profile built about you, and no third party reading your transactions.

The backup feature is entirely optional and entirely under your control. When you switch it on, kharcha creates an encrypted copy of your ledger and stores it on Render. Encryption happens before anything leaves your computer, so the backup service only ever holds scrambled data that it cannot read. You choose when to back up, and you can delete the stored copy whenever you like. If you prefer to keep everything on your own device, simply leave the backup switched off.

🧭 Everyday Workflow

After the first session, kharcha becomes almost invisible. Bank messages arrive as usual, and the ledger quietly updates in the background. A realistic weekly routine looks like this:

  • Once a week: open the ledger and glance at the most recent entries. Correct anything that looks off.
  • Once a month: open the summary view and look at the total for the month. Compare it with last month and note which category grew.
  • Whenever you like: run a backup if you have enabled it, especially before changing computers or reinstalling anything.

That is the entire maintenance routine. There are no reports to generate, no files to email yourself, and no formulas to fix.

❓ Questions People Ask Before Installing

Do I need to create an account?

No. kharcha does not ask for an email address, password, or bank login. You install it and start using it. The optional backup is the only part that involves any outside service, and only if you enable it.

Will it connect to my bank?

No. kharcha never logs into your bank and never needs your banking credentials. It only reads the alert messages your bank already sends you. That keeps your accounts out of reach of the app entirely.

Can I use it on more than one computer?

You can install it wherever you need it. If you want the same ledger on two machines, enable the encrypted backup on the first one and restore it on the second. Without backup, each installation keeps its own separate history.

What happens if I stop using it?

Your ledger stays on your device until you delete the app or clear its data. If you used the backup, you can also delete the stored encrypted copy at any time. Nothing lingers in the background.

Is there a cost?

kharcha is free to download and use. There is no subscription, no premium tier, and no advertising. The optional backup runs on your own Render setup, so its cost, if any, is whatever that service charges you.
